Upforit Cloudinary delivery optimisation — 20 October 2026

Scope
Read contract.md and repository instructions, plus the Cloudinary account audit
and adjacent delivery/inventory evidence in Good Game Apparel. Only Upforit was
edited. Existing unrelated work was preserved. No originals were overwritten or
deleted, and no bulk derivative regeneration was performed.

Changes
- cloudinaryVideoUrl now delivers a full-HD c_limit web encode using q_auto:best,
  H.264 for MP4 / VP9 for WebM. Explicit cloudinaryOriginalVideoUrl retains the
  original URL for future master/download consumers. Existing audio download
  behaviour and links are unchanged.
- MusicHub audio/video use preload=none. Posters load within 200px of the viewport.
  Hidden audio/video cards do not mount media players.
- MerchImage applies its bounded delivery step AFTER upstream crops/effects and
  before the version/public ID. Repeated application replaces its own terminal
  step; widths are finite and capped at 960. Exact Cloudinary host matching;
  signed/token and other-provider URLs pass through unchanged.
- Contract updated. Existing CloudinaryImage and NYE texture preload preserved.

text, fine waveform edges and colour. Fast seeking into the initial fragmented

was fetched from the final cached encode for the conclusive comparison.
cached-compare-210.jpg: master left, cached encode right. Visible logo/text detail

frame, not a whole-video quality score or proof of perceptual equivalence.
The downloaded stream retained AAC stereo at 48kHz. No formal listening comparison
was conducted. WebM generation was URL-tested, not fetched or playback-tested.

Limits and release state
Progressive web delivery was chosen to keep native playback and a conservative
quality setting without introducing an adaptive manifest/player lifecycle or
generating a full bitrate ladder. This is not adaptive streaming. The 66.3% saving
is a complete-file comparison, not a claim about future monthly account usage.
