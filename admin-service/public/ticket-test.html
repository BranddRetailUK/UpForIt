Rehearsal checklist

  1. Pay in Stripe Checkout, then verify the customer confirmation page and order total.
  2. Open the delivered ticket email. Check the attachment, QR codes, and account download.
  3. Scan the QR using the door device. Paid test tickets consume admission even before event day. Scan it again with a new scan request: it must be rejected.
  4. Check every admission has a different ticket number and QR. For the group offer, buy four admissions (£30 at the current NYE price) and verify all four are issued, including the free admission. Check any enabled merchandise entitlement in the customer account.
  5. Use Resend to check delivery controls. An immediate repeat must be rejected.
  6. Open this order’s payment in Stripe and issue a full refund. Refresh until the order reads refunded and tickets read void; scan again to confirm rejection.

Sent means the provider accepted the email; delivered requires its delivery callback. Confirm inbox receipt yourself. No Meta purchase conversion is sent for these admin tests. Tickets and revenue are real records; refund after testing. Returning from Checkout without paying does not cancel the session—use Cancel unpaid checkout below.
